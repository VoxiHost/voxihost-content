## Support hours

The team is available **12:00-20:00 CET**, with best-effort support outside those hours.
Tickets are the supported channel for account, billing and technical issues; include enough
detail to avoid a round trip.

## Create a ticket

1. Open **Support** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Open New Ticket**.
3. Choose a **Department**: Technical Support, Billing & Payments, Sales Department or Other
   Inquiry.
4. Choose a **Priority**: Low, Medium, High or Emergency. Use Emergency only for outages or
   security incidents.
5. Enter a **Subject** and a **Message**.
6. Select **Submit Ticket**.

The ticket appears under **Active** and can be opened from the list.

## Ticket statuses

| Status | Meaning | Who acts next |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | New ticket, no reply yet | Support |
| **Customer-Reply** | You replied and support has not answered yet | Support |
| **Answered** | Support answered | You |
| **Closed** | The ticket is finished and read-only | No one; open a new ticket if needed |

The sidebar badge counts tickets that need your attention.

## Reply and attachments

Open the ticket to see the conversation timeline. Type in the reply box and select send;
**Enter** sends the message and **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. Use the paperclip to
attach screenshots or logs.

Attachments should be relevant and free of secrets. Never attach identity documents,
passwords, API keys or full card numbers; support will ask through a secure channel if it
needs them.

## Close a ticket and download the transcript

- **Download Transcript** saves the full conversation for your records.
- **Close Ticket** ends the thread; a closed ticket becomes read-only.

Download the transcript before closing if you may need the history later. If the issue
returns, open a new ticket and reference the old number.

## What to include

Good tickets get faster answers. Include:

- The instance name and public IP, or the account email.
- The exact error message or status text.
- When the problem started and whether anything changed at that time.
- Which browser or client you used, if the panel itself misbehaves.
- Relevant screenshots or log excerpts.

For billing issues, add the transaction date, amount and document number. For firewall
issues, state the source address and whether the Firewall Block Analyzer shows a cause.
