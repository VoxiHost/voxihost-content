## How the program works

You earn commission when people you refer create an account and top up Credits through your
referral link. Commission is calculated on **every settled deposit**, not only the first one.

- The deposit must be worth at least **20 Credits** to qualify.
- The rate is the commission rate set on your affiliate account; the default is **10%**.
- Commission is credited to your affiliate balance and appears in the affiliate history.

The panel tracks **Total Clicks**, **Registered Users**, **Total Earned** and **Balance**.
Clicks are recorded when visitors arrive through your referral link; the attribution window
and eligibility details follow the program rules: ask support for edge cases.

## Your referral link

The **Promotion** section contains your personal referral link with a copy button. Share that
link: a visit to the plain website URL is not attributed to you.

## Earnings and balance

Each qualifying deposit adds commission to your affiliate balance. The **Affiliate History**
table lists the events with pagination; when there is nothing yet it shows **Nothing to show**.

Commission is denominated in Credits, the same unit as the wallet.

## Withdrawing

**Withdraw Now** moves the entire affiliate balance into your **main Credit balance**: it is
not a cash payout.

1. Check the **Balance** on the Affiliates page.
2. Select **Withdraw Now**.
3. Confirm. The minimum is **10.00 Credits**.

The success message confirms the transfer to your main balance, and the affiliate balance is
reset to zero. If the withdrawal fails, confirm the balance meets the minimum and try again;
if it keeps failing, open a support ticket.

## Rules

- **Self-referral is prohibited.** Using affiliate links for your own purchases, or creating
  fake accounts to claim commission, forfeits the accumulated affiliate balance.
- Only qualifying deposits earn commission; the rate is the one set on your account.
- Program terms, including the rate, eligibility and payout rules, can change; the panel
  and the Terms of Service are authoritative.
- For a disputed referral or missing commission, open a support ticket with the referral
  account email and the deposit date.
